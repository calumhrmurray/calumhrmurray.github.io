SPHEREx  ·  the cosmic web

The half sky, through redshift

The half-sky SPHEREx catalogue, scrolled through redshift. Each frame is a shell of thickness Δz = 0.03, holding every galaxy whose calibrated probability of lying in the shell is above 0.7; the shell’s centre steps by 0.0025, so consecutive frames overlap and the web drifts rather than flickers. Click the map to pause it on a shell. It runs from 0.02 < z < 0.05 out to 0.32 < z < 0.35.

It starts on the shell shown in the application’s figure, 0.07 < z < 0.10: 512,148 galaxies over the 20,800 deg² the catalogue covers, 9,300 deg² of which is sky that neither DESI nor SDSS maps.

Drawn from the 21,200,305 quality, non-stellar, fitted galaxies of the SPHEREx all-sky catalogue (galaxies_v2_allsky_v1). Calum Murray.
